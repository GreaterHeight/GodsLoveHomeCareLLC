Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains the browser storage and optional analytics used by the God's Love Home Care, LLC website.

Essential storage

This website stores your cookie preference in your browser's local storage so that it can remember whether you accepted or rejected optional analytics. This storage is necessary to operate the preference control and does not require an analytics choice.

Analytics

Optional analytics are off unless you choose to allow them. If the site owner configures a Google Analytics 4 measurement ID, the analytics script may load only after you give consent. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the Cookie settings control in the website footer.

Marketing cookies

This website does not currently activate marketing cookies through its cookie preference control.

Managing your preferences

Use the Accept all, Reject optional, or Preferences controls in the cookie notice. You can reopen the preference panel using Cookie settings in the footer. Clearing your browser's site data also clears the saved preference.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact info@godslovehomecarellc.com.

Important: This policy describes the controls currently implemented on this static website. It should be reviewed by the business for legal accuracy and updated if additional analytics, embedded media, advertising, or third-party services are introduced.
